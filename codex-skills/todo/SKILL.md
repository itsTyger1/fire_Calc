---
name: todo
description: Produce a complete checked checklist of all completed and outstanding steps in the current Codex session.
---

When the user invokes `/todo`, reconstruct the current task from the conversation and workspace evidence.

- Include every meaningful completed step as a checked item.
- Include every outstanding step as an unchecked item.
- Separate completed and outstanding items when that improves clarity.
- Mention blockers, pending decisions, and verification gaps.
- Do not invent steps or mark work complete without evidence.
