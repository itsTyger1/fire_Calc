---
name: tldr
description: Summarize the immediately preceding answer or result in exactly three short bullet points, each under 10 words.
---

When the user invokes `/tldr`, summarize the immediately preceding assistant answer or completed result.

- Output exactly three bullet points.
- Keep every bullet under 10 words.
- Preserve the most important facts, decisions, and next action.
- Do not add a preamble, caveat, heading, or extra bullets.
