---
name: remind
description: Reconstruct the purpose, progress, decisions, and next step of the current Codex session.
---

When the user invokes `/remind`, summarize the current session from the conversation and workspace context.

Include:

- What the session is about.
- What has been completed.
- Important decisions, assumptions, or blockers.
- The next concrete step.

Be concise, candid about uncertainty, and do not invent progress.
