Historical prompt for the original beige design. The current white tiger and burnt orange edit is documented in `fire-tiger-white-orange-prompt.md`; the current source artwork is `fire-tiger-minimal.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: style-transfer

Asset type: final square iPhone Home Screen app icon for FIRE Projector.

Input image 1 is the current tiger-and-flame app icon to redesign.

Primary request: make this icon much more minimal and aesthetic with a pure black background. Think restrained editorial design, warm quiet aesthetics, sophisticated flat graphic simplicity. Preserve the identity of a forward-facing tiger head with a small integrated flame, but dramatically simplify into a bold elegant abstract emblem, clear at 48 pixels. Use a single warm ivory/tan color (#d8c5ab) for a few solid shapes and black negative space to suggest ears, eyes, nose, tiger stripes, and one subtle flame above the head. Flat vector-like graphic, balanced bilateral symmetry, calm expression, no realistic fur, no neon orange, no red, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no texture, no text, no letters, no border. Large intentional negative space. Emblem takes about 60 percent of width, centered on opaque full-bleed pure black (#000000) square canvas. Do not copy Claude's logo or any other brand logo. Render only the icon artwork, not an iPhone mockup.
